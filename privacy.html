Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: mSpendVanta doesn't collect your personal information. Your financial data is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud account, where we can't see it. We don't run servers that receive your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. If you use AI suggestions with Apple's Private Cloud Compute, the text of a transaction is sent to Apple to suggest a vendor and category — never to us.

This policy explains how mSpendVanta for Mac and the mSpendVanta companion app for iPhone (together, "the app"), made by Meg Software ("we", "us"), handle information. It also covers this website.

Your financial data

The app works with information you choose to give it: the statement files you download from your bank or credit card company (OFX, QFX or CSV), and the accounts, transactions, vendors, categories, rules, budgets and bills you create.

  • It stays on your devices. Files you import are read on your Mac. The app never asks for, stores or uses your bank passwords, and it doesn't connect to your bank.
  • It syncs through your own iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, your data is stored in a private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple Account, so it can sync between your Mac and iPhone. Only you can access it — we can't. iCloud is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • We never receive it. We don't operate servers that receive, store or process your financial data.

Suggestions powered by Apple Intelligence

On Macs that support Apple Intelligence, the app can suggest a vendor and category for your transactions. You choose how, in the app's Settings under AI Suggestions:

  • Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Apple's larger model, running on Apple's servers, makes the suggestions. To do this, the app sends Apple the transaction's description and memo; the descriptions of a few similar past transactions, with the vendor and category you gave them; and the names of your vendors and categories so the model can choose from them. Amounts, dates, balances and your account details aren't sent, and before sending, the app removes phone numbers and the cardholder names and account numbers some banks include in descriptions. Apple uses this information only to answer the request, doesn't store it, and doesn't make it available to anyone, including Apple. Private Cloud Compute is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; you can read how it protects your data in Apple's Private Cloud Compute overview. This option is on by default on Macs where it's available.
  • On your Mac only. Apple's on-device model makes the suggestions entirely on your Mac, and nothing leaves it. The app also uses this model when Private Cloud Compute is turned off, isn't available, or has reached Apple's daily limit.
  • Off. You can turn suggestions off altogether and categorize your transactions yourself.

Whichever you choose, transaction details are never sent to us.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm what you've purchased, the app checks your purchase records with Apple's StoreKit on your device. Apple provides us with sales reports that don't identify you personally. Apple's handling of your purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share personal information.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can change this anytime in your device's privacy settings.

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to help with your question, and we don't add you to any mailing list. Please don't send us your statement files or account numbers.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. The site is hosted by GitHub; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete accounts, transactions and other items in the app. Data stored in iCloud can also be removed from your Apple Account's iCloud storage settings. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on that device; data in iCloud remains until you delete it there.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email us at Meg Software.
