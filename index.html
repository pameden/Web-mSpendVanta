A simple routine that gets quicker every time

Most people repeat this once a week or so. Once your rules are set up, it takes just a few minutes.

  1. Download

    Download statement files from your bank's and credit card's websites. OFX and QFX (Quicken) files work best, and CSV is supported too.

  2. Import

    Import them into mSpendVanta. New accounts are set up for you, your current balance is picked up, and transactions you've already imported are skipped.

  3. Categorize

    Match new transactions to a vendor and a category. Rules remember your choices, so each import sorts itself a little more than the last.

  4. Review

    See your spending, how you're doing against your budget, what bills are coming up and where your balances are heading — on your Mac and your iPhone.
